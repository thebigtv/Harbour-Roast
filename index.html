Our story

Coffee rooted in community

"We started The Harbour Roast because we believed great coffee deserved a great place to enjoy it."

Founded in 2018 by two friends who fell in love with speciality coffee on a trip to Melbourne, The Harbour Roast has grown from a tiny kiosk into a full kitchen-and-café serving the local community.

We work directly with small farms, change our single-origin offerings every season, and roast in small batches to keep every cup tasting exactly as it should.
